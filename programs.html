Program 02 · Allergens listed

Chilled, frozen & food to go

Anything prepacked for direct sale here carries a full ingredient list with allergens emphasised, as the law requires. If an allergy is serious, ask us at the counter rather than relying on a label alone, because we can tell you what is handled nearby.

Program 03 · Challenge 25, no exceptions

Age-restricted products

We ask anyone who looks under 25 for photographic ID, and we refuse without it. We also refuse proxy sales — buying alcohol for someone under 18 is a criminal offence for the adult doing it, and the till is not the place to argue about that. Refusals are logged.

  • Licensed hours
  • Over 18s
  • Per item
  • Photographic ID
